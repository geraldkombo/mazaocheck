# MazaoCheck

A free web tool that shows Kenyan farmers the official KNBS/AFA government crop price next to the typical broker price — closing the **Price Truth Gap** so a fair price can fund healthier soil, animals, and families.

**Live:** https://geraldkombo.github.io/mazaocheck/

## Why it matters
Smallholders sell at throw-away farm-gate prices because they price off rumours, not bulletins. A fair price is the upstream determinant of One Health: it funds organic inputs (ecosystem), vet care (animal), and good food (human). One number, three kinds of health.

## Features
- 28 crops including 9 indigenous varieties (managu, terere, kunde, mrenda, saga, ndengu, cassava, arrow roots, sweet potatoes)
- Red broker price vs green government price, with exact loss in KES and %
- Agroecology tip and One Health line per crop result
- Bilingual (English + Swahili), works on any phone, no install
- Farmer price reports across all 47 counties — honest county-level price evidence
- CSV export of all reports
- Printable Swahili Price Truth Card for markets and feature-phone farmers
- Supabase pooling (optional) for multi-device report consolidation

## Data honesty
Government prices are official KNBS/AFA references. Broker prices are farmer-reported field quotes, growing as more farmers report. No fabricated numbers.

## Built for
The 3rd Kenya National Agroecology Symposium — Call for Innovations: Scaling Agroecological Solutions for One Health Approach. Aligned with Kenya's National Agroecology Strategy (2024–2033).

Built independently by Gerald Kombo, Nairobi. Ready to scale through PELUM Kenya's network of 63 member organisations across all 47 counties.
